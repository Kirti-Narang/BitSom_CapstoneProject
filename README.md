# Mosaic Trip

From inspiration to execution, without the planning chaos.

A multi-agent AI travel planning system built as a team capstone for the BITSoM x Masai programme, Product Management with Generative & Agentic AI (Dec 2025 – Jul 2026).

## Problem Statement

How can the process of travel planning be simplified by designing a multi-agent system that handles research, bookings, budget optimisation and contextual recommendations?

User reviews of leading online travel platforms on Reddit, X, LinkedIn and Facebook point to three recurring problems:
- Hotel bookings that do not match reality, including listings with no property at the location
- Delayed, denied or voucher-only refunds
- Customer support that loops through automated replies without reaching a person

## Solution

An orchestrator agent coordinates four specialised agents:

| Agent | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Research | Destinations, travel seasons, visa rules, weather and attractions |
| Booking | Compares flights, hotels and activities across platforms |
| Budget Optimisation | Allocates budget across flights, stays, food and experiences |
| Contextual Recommendation | Personalises suggestions by trip purpose, companions and local events |

## Target Customers

- Young travellers and weekend explorers
- Family vacation travellers
- Pilgrimage and cultural travellers
- Adventure and trekking enthusiasts

## Market Sizing (Phase 1 estimates)

- Total addressable market: approximately USD 250 Bn (Indian travel market)
- Serviceable addressable market: approximately USD 50 Bn (online travel)
- Serviceable obtainable market: approximately USD 1 Bn target within 6 years
